Aeon Game Design Document

OVERVIEW

The Aeon design document is based on an exercise for students applying to the CNAM-ENJMIN (one of the best game design schools in France), so I thought it would be a good exercise to practice game design. The challenge is to outline every aspect of a game in only 10 pages.

Aeon is a story-based, exploration, puzzle-platformer, adventure game. The story revolves around an explorer and their companion trying to find an ancient artifact to save their home. The player takes control of the explorer and is tasked with traversing and exploring the dangers of the outside world as well as solving puzzles in order to reach the artifact.

Writing Aeon’s document challenged me to work under constraints. I thought about every aspect of game development, rangeing from creating a story and world, to the artistic direction the game should take, the specific game principles and gameplay loop, while tailoring the experience to its core audience.

The story and world created were inspired by the prompt, "Traveler, There Is No Path" by Antonio Machado, where it talks about a traveller leaving footprints behind creatig a path for someone. I really was inspired by the idea of leading a path forward for those who come after you (I realize now that this was a good idea due to the sucess of Clair Obscur: Expedition 33, having a similar theme). \ The icy enviroment came from wanting an environment that would have prominent footsteps, as well as the fact that tundras are beautiful varying landscapes that would allow for numerous traversal mechanics.

The 3D exploration with 2D seggments was a creative decision to indicate to the player that 3D is the traversal mode, while 2D is puzzle and story game mode.
