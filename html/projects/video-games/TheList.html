OVERVIEW

An intership for designing the levels of a daily trivia game called TheList developed by the New York based studio LocalNo.12! The game is meant to tap into the daily games market & trivia games, testing the player's knowledge of pop culture and other topics!

What I did

The other intern and I were tasked with researching, developing, playtesting, implementing and finalizing over 130+ levels, with both us implementing around 60+ levels each. Each level consists of 3 lists escalating in size (a list of 5, a list of 7, and a list of 10) and an overarching theme to connect all of the lists together.

While working on the project I had found that we had no efficient check for any similar or duplicate topics for all of the lists, nor a way to keep track of how many topics we covered with each list. In response to this I took the initiative to make an excel sheet and transfer over the data from the in-house game engine, and then manually assign different categories for each list. The excel sheet allowed us keep track of the distribution of categories, as well as adding functionality to search the differnt lists and ensure each idea was unique. This excel sheet was then used to implement all the list data back into the in-house engine we were using for the project.

Initially the internship was supposed to only last a month but our employers were happy with our work and extended it by a few months.
