OVERVIEW

A TableTop Role Playing Game (TTRPG) inspired by the Gears of War franchise created to emulate the video games in RPG format. The game is an action heavy game, with tactical squad based combat, the system runs on a 2d8 system with exploding dice. The goal of the game is to make a straight forward tactical TTRPG, that anyone can pick up and play the game. The system was heavily inspired by Pathfinder 2e and SWADE.

My duties included:

  • Research and gain a deep understanding of the Gears of War IP, to replicate the video games in Tabletop format
  • Design, develop, and playtest a core rule set, a starter adventure, pre-made characters, a starter campaign, and a condensted version of the rulebook
  • Balancing & playtesting to ensure variance in gameplay, and that all character options, and weapons are balanced around each other
  • Update content with new releases with fan requested content, as well as content from the newest video game release

System Design

The focus of the game is combat, which is core to the franchise. I designed a 3 Action Point combat economy, where players must work in tandem to defeat an onslaught of enemies. Player progression occurs through building up skills and gaining Perks (unique skills that can play off of each), as after every session players recieve experience points, rather than gaining Levels.

Classes exist as specializations, granting characters unique abilities and differing starting equipment based on what they choose. There is a mix of offensive, defensive, and supporting specializations for all types of players. For example, the Marksman class passive ability is to take a reduced penalty to aimed shots, incentivizing the player to use the action more often. An example weapon would be the GZR18 Markza, which when proficient enough grants a bonus to aimed shots. With these two examples you can see a synergy between potential weapons and classes, creating various options for the players to experiment and play with.

The game has 50+ weapons all with differing special abilities, as well as 25+ unique enemy units. The game's balance for the weapons, enemies, and overall pacing of the game was achieved through spreadsheet and multiple playtest sessions.

Dice Distribution

The decision to use 2d8 was backed by a lot of calculations on dice distribution, as well as doing research on various other TTRPG systems to see how they use their dice to supplement their mechanics. The reason I chose on having 2 dice is because it allows for controlled but varied results when rolling for tasks, as the results become a standard distribution, instead of a random chance for each result. 2d8 specifically allow for a wide range of results, while not being too unpredictable and swingy that a traditional d20 has.

Narrative Design

For the project I wrote, edited, and ran multiple gameplay scenarios. I started by created a campaign, a starter adventure, pre-made characters with backstories, as well writing and editing the core rule book for this game. The pre-made characters are an easy entry point for players who want to just quickly try out the game and not spend all the time needed to make characters.

For all of the adventure modules, I split the campaign into 5 differenct acts, with 5 chapters each inspired by how the video game franchise's campaign is structured. The structure was also inspired by the Beat charts in RTalsorian Game's Cyberpunk books, explaining how to structure and run a TTRPG game. Each module has a recap at the start of each section, that way the Game Master doesn't need to re-read all of the book to understand what is happening and what they are running.

The content of each story is strucutred similarly to how it is done in the games. Typically the player will have a grand mission, going from point A to point B with obstacles inbetween. I always tried to vary the types of encoutners the player would have to face, as constant combat can get tedious and boring fast.
