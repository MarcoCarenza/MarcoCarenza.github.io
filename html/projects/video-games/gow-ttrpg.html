Duration: Ongoing
Team size: 1

Find on Itch.io

OVERVIEW

A TableTop Role Playing Game (TTRPG) inspired by the Gears of War franchise created to emulate the video games in RPG format. The game is an action heavy gakme, with tactical squad based combat, the system is ran on a 2d8 system with exploding dice. The goal of the game is to make a straight forward tactical TTRPG, so that anyone can pick up and play the game. The system was heavily inspired by pathfinder 2e and SWADE.

System Design

The focus of the game is combat, which is core to the franchise. I designed a 3 Action Point combat economy, where players must work in tandem to defeat an onslaught of enemies. Player progression occurs through building up skills and gaining Perks, as after every session players recieve experience points, rather than gaining Levels. Classes exist as specializations, granting characters unique abilities and differing starting equipment based on what they choose. There is a mix of offensive, defensive, and supporting specializations for all types of players. The game has 50+ weapons all with differing special abilities, as well as 25+ unique enemy units. The game's balance for the weapons, enemies, and overall pacing of the game was achieved through spreadsheet and multiple playtest sessions.

Dice Distribution

The decision to use 2d8 was backed by a lot of calculations on dice distribution, as well as doing research on various other TTRPG systems to see how they use their dice to supplement their mechanics. The reason I chose on having 2 die makes it a allows for controlled but varied results when rolling for tasks, as the results become a standard distribution, instead of a random chance for each result. 2d8 specifically allow for a wide range of results, while not being to unpredictable and swingy that a traditional d20 has.

Narrative Design

For the project I wrote, edited, and ran multiple gameplay scenarios. I started by created a campaign, a starter adventure, pre-made characters with backstories, as well writing and editing the core rule book for this game. For all of the adventure modules, I split the campaign into 5 differenct acts, with 5 chapters each inspired by how the video game franchise's campaign is structured. The structure was also inspired by the Beat charts in RTalsorian Game's Cyberpunk books, explaining how to structure and run a TTRPG game.
